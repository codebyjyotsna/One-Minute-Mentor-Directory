# One-Minute-Mentor-Directory
**One-Minute Mentor** is a web application where users can browse, upload, and engage with 1-minute mentoring videos in the fields of Tech, Design, and Business. The platform allows mentors to share their knowledge through short video clips, while viewers can filter videos by categories, search for specific topics, and follow their favorite mentors.
## Features
### For Users:
- **Browse Videos**: Easily explore a directory of 1-minute mentoring videos.
- **Search & Filter**: Search videos by title, tags, or categories (e.g., Tech, Design, Business).
- **Follow Mentors**: Follow mentors to stay updated on their latest uploads.

### For Mentors:
- **Video Uploads**: Upload 1-minute videos with titles, descriptions, tags, and categories.
- **Engagement**: Get likes and analytics on your videos to track impact.
